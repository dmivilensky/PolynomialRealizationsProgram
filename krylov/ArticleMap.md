# krylov: article correspondence

Numbers, labels and titles follow [the article](../krylov.pdf).
Names are relative to `krylov`.
[Article.lean](Article.lean) checks the same declarations in the same order;
its numbered comments explain the hypotheses, normalizations and quantifiers.
Definitions are checked as definitions. Composite claims list the declarations
and dictionaries needed for all their conclusions.

The printed statements have been compared with the actual Lean signatures.
The reviewed counts and the Lean showcase let the shared checker enforce
the recorded ordered inventory and per-block references. This records the
mathematical correspondence; the kernel checks the referenced proofs.
