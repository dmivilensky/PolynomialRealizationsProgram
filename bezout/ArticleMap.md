# bezout: mathematical correspondence

This map covers all mathematical results of
[Chebyshev-Exact Acceleration under Gradient Variation](../bezout.pdf):
Theorems 2.1, 3.1 and 4.1, Lemmas C.1 and C.2, Proposition D.1,
Algorithms 1 and 2, and the supporting unnumbered constructions,
probability identities, models and analytic comparisons.
The signatures have been compared with the article's quantifiers,
hypotheses, normalizations and actual oracle execution semantics.
[Article.lean](Article.lean) checks the listed signatures in article order;
[Check.lean](Check.lean) audits all owned sources and proof dependencies.

Concrete experimental dimensions, selected numerical parameters, decimal
illustrations, observed results, figures, Monte Carlo/bootstrap calculations
and bibliographic history are excluded. Their general mathematical models
are proved with symbolic dimensions, amplitudes, endpoint masses and noise
parameters. Exact horizon formulas and rational constants are retained.
A statement imported from an earlier package is connected to the actual
history or physical program used here; no numerical certificate archive is
needed. Equivalent direct proofs replace auxiliary proof devices in the
signed-mass optimizer argument and the auxiliary parity matrix factorization,
as explained below. The map records mathematical correspondence, while
compilation and axiom replay separately check the proofs.
